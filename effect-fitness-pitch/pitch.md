Core features, key flows, and what the MVP looks like.

## Business Model

## Competition
Who else is in this space, and what's the edge over them?

## Go-to-Market
How do you plan to acquire the first users?

## Traction / Validation
Any early signals — waitlist, prototype feedback, pilot users?

## Team
Who's building this, and why are they the right team?

## Ask
